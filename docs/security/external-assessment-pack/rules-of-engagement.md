# Rules of engagement template

- Sponsor, assessor and emergency contacts: **operator supplied**
- Authorized origins/IPs and tenant: **operator supplied**
- Start/end time and timezone: **operator supplied**
- Maximum intensity: low, non-destructive
- Synthetic data only; no production credentials
- No social engineering, persistence, malware, destructive action or public
  third-party targeting
- Stop immediately on scope ambiguity, tenant crossover, data exposure or
  emergency-stop instruction
- Encrypt evidence in transit and at rest; retain only for the agreed period
- Report urgent findings immediately through the named secure channel

Approval requires dated signatures from the sponsor and assessor before work.
